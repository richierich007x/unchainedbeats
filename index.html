The Artist

RICHARD DARWIN
CANTOS JR.

Born in Oceanside, California. Forged in the desert heat of Yucca Valley. Richard Darwin Cantos Jr. — performing as Richie_Rich007 — is a Hybrid Artist who refuses to be boxed in by genre.

From the raw screams of emo punk rock to the seductive whispers of R&B, from dark country honky-tonks to packed dance floors — his catalog of 14+ albums covers every emotion, every story, every scar.

"Old MP3 ghosts from when I was 16, remastered through AI fire. This is the rawest version of me."
